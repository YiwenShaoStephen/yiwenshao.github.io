ABOUT

I'm currently a Founding Research Scientist at Nuance Labs, building full-duplex multimodal LLMs.

Previously Senior Researcher at Tencent AI Lab & Hunyuan (Bellevue) under Dong Yu (俞栋) — led audio-language pretraining and representation learning, founded Auden, key contributor to the initial launch of Hunyuan Speech 1.0, and led audio pretraining for Hunyuan Speech 3.0.

Ph.D. from JHU CLSP, advised by Daniel Povey and Sanjeev Khudanpur — focused on ASR and audio understanding.
